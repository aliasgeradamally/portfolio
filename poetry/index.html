Letters to the feelings that refused to stay quiet.

Invisible String Theory

Have you heard of the invisible string theory? A theory based on fate, destiny, and determinism. A theory about qismat. A theory that suggests that both of us are connected, by an invisible string, like puppets in a greater play. And what it means is that no matter what, we will eventually find our way back to each other. We kind of did though, didn’t we? Or maybe that hasn’t happened yet.. I can’t see the invisible string, but I’m starting to feel like I’m getting tangled in something.. Is it the string? or is something else at play? What is this feeling? I don’t know that it’s our turn yet.. but still, I can’t break this feeling, that something is in action, forces not in my control, definitely not in my jurisdiction. Whether it is, or whether isn’t.. we might still have some time. But now you’ve also heard of the invisible string theory. The theory based on fate, destiny, determinism, and well, us.

The reason I write..

I write, just to get a glimpse, of this overwhelming feeling, this exhilarating dealing. My words, put it into effect, a mesmerizing sight, one as ravishing as the night. What glimpse? What sight? As liberating as the sun, yet a comforting one. It’s you. In my words I see, revealing through the lines, as stunning as the rhymes. Like saying ‘Hi’ for the first time, but day in and out, these words stay with me, even when I’m out and about. It’s a reminder for sure, something to miss, oh so true. When I write my lines, whenever I do, it’s simple really, its all for a glimpse of you

I long for those stories to tell

How I long for those stories to tell A tale in which the brothers dwell Stories of mischief, trouble and glee Nonsense and shenanigans of a different degree Of course it’s the people, everyone agrees But people can change, so what’s the quandary Only in that land, these dreams come true Only on that soil, bonds like these brew Where the brothers grew and formed their bond Is that the difference, why these friendships spawned? I beg for a chance, please here my plea If it’s up to me, then hear my decree Why must our bonds be riddled with hurt Do these stories alone not prove the worth- of brotherhoods so strong that whatever may be Separated by the family, the work, the sea These bonds can never go sour, there isn’t a chance Insults and forgiveness without a second glance Friendships formed over a common crime Friendships so true they stand the test of time Friendships that forever stay in their prime Late night drives and painting the town In this day and age who wouldn’t give a frown A wavelength so in tune, a longing for fun so alike Meeting up was simple for them, wonder what that would be like But when all is said and done, and I bid these yearnings farewell For we grew up like this, while they used to play hell Sure I have friends as well With many more years and adventures yet to befell But even then, at this ripe young age, oh how I long for those stories to tell

Even I don’t know me.

They said ‘he’s changed’ No doubt about that In what way though I’m dying to know Of course I changed It wasn’t a one night exchange I changed when they changed me But they don’t know that And yet they make me feel guilty What do they know about me? They can’t even spell my name correctly They’re so busy even when they reach out they say it How the hell would they know what my problem is And yet it’s me sitting here, in my own self pity Even I don’t know me I think I used to be happy And now my expression stays glee-free Is that what changed? It wasn’t a one night exchange I changed when they pushed me away It wasn’t just a play Or a really bad phase I wasn’t in a good place I needed a hand and embrace But the wounds were left to decay I found solace in another way But that rift had already made its name They don’t know this.. Even I don’t know me Now I get angry easily When I look around, the fire always burns atop my head Even though it was lit elsewhere, The smoke gets too thick to see through I always end up burnt I don’t disagree that I lit up Maybe that’s where I changed But it wasn’t a one night exchange Why won’t anyone else ‘fess up It’s just a spiral of Condescending Hypocritical Biased Faithless people No faith in what I’m to be or what I try to do Even I don’t know me My attitude, my personality Is it a bad change They act like I’m a dark cloud Impossible to tame Always playing a different game How am I supposed to show that this change, It wasn’t a one night exchange I didn’t choose this mode This difficulty level doesn’t sit right with me But they don’t actually see They just judge and say things of a harsh degree And I’m not talking about just an anomaly I’m sick of giving apology after apology Cuz I’m not sorry anymore But then again what do I actually know When even I don’t know me So I sit here writing this Wondering what I am How I am Who I am And I have to say.. I really don’t know. Now.. if I don’t even know me Then how the hell would they?

Aisa lagta tha

کیا اس مے میرا کوئی گناہ ہے؟ کہ پيار کے باره مے اس طره کی سوچ آتی ہے، جب وہ خوابوں کے علاقے مے، ہر وصل مے وھی انداز دکھتا ہے۔ ان خیالوں کے درمیان کھو نہیں جانا تھا، اب صرف محبت کی خوشبو آتی ہے، اور لمحے یوں بہ جاتے ہے، نہ خوبصورتی کی قیمت، نہ درد کے کانٹے، بس معصومیت سے بھری ہوئی ہے ہر ملاقاتیں۔ جب دل کے لفظ قدر کی بات کرتے تھے، اور محبت کا ہر پل عبادت سا لگتا تھا، الفاظ مے نہیں کہہ سکتا ہوں، وہ سچا والا عشق مے کیسا نیة تھے۔ تو کیااس مے میرا کوئی گناہ ہے، جب وہ زمانے مے محبت ایسا لگتا تھا-

Shukranlillah

Shukranlillah, for the gift of sound, To hear the laugh where my joy is found. Notes that linger, so pure and clear, A melody my heart holds oh so near. Shukranlillah, for the gift of sight, To have witnessed a smile, so perfect under the light. Like a star that signals in the dark, It's like a strength, an everlasting spark. Shukranlillah, for the words to say, To form words and feelings, for me to pray. For control against saying words that may be overdue, Yet the freedom to speak what is soft and true. Shukranlillah, for love unspoken, Although split, never broken. Every moment feels like another sign, Shukranlillah for trusting me with what is now mine.

Sukoon

I think I found it At the end of a tiring chase It was under my nose, literally In a most obvious place In the grand scheme of things, I now appreciate Your grace You need not speak directly to me Yet I still feel Your embrace Countless dua, A neverending stream of wants I thank You for listening For all of Your grants I never feel alone It’s almost like a trance And now I can see How everything enchants But I thank You most for showing me light For giving me my takat and might For letting me believe in what’s right For giving me colour in black and white Thank you for the sukoon, in my every night

I don’t..

I don’t just miss you in the middle of the night, or at 2am I miss you even in the most crowded of rooms, even with my bestest friend I don’t just see you in old pictures, or recalled memories I see you in the randomest of places, a flowery garden, or the pretty seas I don’t just feel your presence when I’m with you, or hearing your voice I feel you in the subtlest moments, in every breath, in every noise I don’t need to try to think of you. You are everything I do and everything around me. You’re my only constant in the vastness of all that is, or all that will ever be.

‘I would’ve waited forever’ without saying ‘I would’ve waited forever’

Aap ke liye main chamakta aasmaan ke sitaare chupa doon, Jab tak har ek sitare ki roshni na chali jaaye, Jab tak waqt ka ek pal bhi na rahe Baw apni kismat ke sannate, Baw apne naseeb ki cheekhein, Main sab kuch de sakta hoon, Magar is chahat ko marne nahi dete

Time

Waqt guzarta rehta hai Majboori mein hum dekhte rehte hain Par jab yeh pal beet jaata hai, Aur kuch karne ka mann ho jaata hai, Kis tarah ka darr lagta hai, Aur phir hum dekhte rehte hain Jab waqt guzarta rehta hai Kyun guzarta rehta hai? Hum kyun dekhte rehte hain? Us pal ko kyun beet jaane dete hain, Aur jab kuch karne ka mann ho jaata hai, Kyun is tarah ka darr lagta hai, Aur hum phir se kyun dekhte rehte hain Jab waqt guzarta rehta hai Kabhi kabhi jab mann ho jaata hai, Toh sochta hoon ke hum kuch karte hain Kya se kya ho jaayenge Kuch na kuch ho jaayenge Kab se, kahaan se, aur kaise ho jaayenge Yeh sab kyun ho jaayenge Kyun ke usi pal mein mohabbat ka izhaar hota hai Usi pal mein mohabbat ki khushboo mehakhti hai Usi pal mein mohabbat ka noor chamakta hai Aur usi pal par sirf mohabbat ka haq hota hai Lekin, phir se, Waqt guzarta rehta hai Majboori mein hum dekhte rehte hain Jab woh pal beet jaata hai, Aur kuch karne ka mann ho jaata hai, Us tarah ka darr lagta hai, Aur hum phir se dekhte rehte hain Jab waqt guzarta rehta hai

Dard

کیا درد ہے یہ؟ تڑپتا جاتا ہے یہ دل، بہت افسوس ہے مجھے، جی رہا ہوں یہ مشکل۔ یہ کیسی بے بسی ہے، ناامید سا ہو رہا ہوں مسلسل۔ کر سکتے تھے آپ یہ چاہت اب میرے، چکھنے تو دے سکتا تھا، میرے صبر کا وہ پھل۔ ایک ہی لمحہ تھے میرے، اور اب بہت دور ہے وہ پل۔ افسوس ہے کہ وہ یادیں سب ہم کو مٹانی چاہئیں، مگر نہیں ہو پائے گے، نہ آج، نہ کل۔
Kya dard hai ye? Tarapta jaata hai ye dil Bahut afsos hai mujhe Jee raha hoon ye mushkil Ye kaisi bebasi hai Na umeed sa ho raha hoon musalsal Kar sakte the aap ye chahat ab mere Chakhne to de sakta tha, mera sabr ka woh phal Ek hi lamhe the mere Aur ab bohot dur hai woh pal Afsos hai ke woh yaadein sab humko mitani chahiye Nahin ho paayege, na aaj na kal

lost and found
yet unretrieved

یہ دل تو کھو چکا ہوں، اب پاس میں نہیں رہتا میرے۔ بہت دور جا کر صرف ڈھونڈنے جاتا ہوں، اور وہیں ملتا ہے جہاں کھو دیا تھا میں نے۔ جب بھی میں اُس کے سامنے آتا ہوں، پھر لینے کا من ہو جاتا ہے۔ پر کیسے میں اُس کو واپس لاؤں، جب اُس کے نصیب میں یہی جگہ لکھی ہے۔ دیکھا جائے تو برسوں ہو گئی ہیں اُس دن کو، پر وہ یاد ابھی تک بھلا نہیں پاؤں۔ خیر، وہ یاد ہی سہی۔ آخر اب ڈھونڈنے نہیں آتا ہوں، بس وہی آکے ملتا ہوں، جہاں کھو دیا تھا میں نے۔

Dooriyan

میلوں دور، پر نزدیک ہیں ہم تیری یاد کرتا ہوں، ہر پل ہر دم آنسو جاری، آنکھیں بہت نم پھر بھی یہ چاہت نہیں ہوتی ہیں کم مجبور ہوں میں، محبت کی قسم میرے دل پہ بھاری ہے یہ دوری کا ستم کاش تم یہی پاس میں ہوتے، بس ایک قدم پھر یہ گہرا محبت کا نہ ہوتا یہ غم میرے دل کی دھڑکن میں تم ہو، کبھی ہو گی نہ ختم دعاء ہے کے بس تیرا میرا ایک ہوجائے جنم وقت کو بدلتے دیکھتا ہوں، ایک دن ہوں گے ہم بھم یہ عرض ہو قبول، اللّٰہ میرے کردے کرم

Pehchaan-e-dil

پہچانا تھا میرے دل نے اک رشتہ بہت پرانا نہیں چھوڑ سکتا اب میں جو مل گیا ہے دوبارہ وقت پھسلنے لگا ہے رہوںگا نہیں میں بس بیچارہ اکیلا یہ سب کرنا مشکل ہے دیجیے نا، اپنا سہارا امیدیں اب چھوٹنے لگی ہیں بس دیجیے مجھے ایک اشارہ دوڑتا دوڑتا آجاؤنگا میں بن جاؤنگا میں صرف تمہارا

Breathless

ذرا بےچین سا لگ رہا ہے ذرا بےسانس کا ہو گیا ہوں آنکھیں میری شیشے جیسی ہو گئی ہیں جو کہ آنسو کی نمی میں سب کچھ دیکھ سکتا ہے تو بہت کچھ سن لیا ہے اب پریشانی میں جئیں گے تمہاری وجہ سے چلیں گے تم میرے سوائے اب اگر اسی وقت میں ہم ایک نہیں ہوں گے انتظار نہیں ہوگا کیا میرے لیے تھوڑی دیر ہی رکنا پڑے گا تم دوڑتا دوڑتا آتا میں پر دوڑتے دوڑتے جانا ہے تم ذرا بےچین سا لگ رہا ہے ذرا بےسانس کا ہو گیا ہوں کیسے چھوڑوں ایک ہی ارمان میرے جاؤ گے تم تو میں کس کے لیے جیوں

Kaun Ho Tum?
